As an ASU Intelligence Fellow, I'm one of about 50 students university-wide in a cohort built around practitioner briefings, analytic exercises, and Intelligence Community speakers. My goal is a technical role where engineering and national security meet. I'm willing to relocate to Washington, D.C.
